# Process

How to make the beer.

This guide is based on brewing with the **Brewzilla 35L**, an all-in-one electric brewing system. It handles heating your water, mashing your grains (via the built-in malt pipe), and boiling your wort -- all in one vessel. No propane, no separate kettles, no brew bags.

The process breaks down into three main phases:

1. **[Brew Day](/process/brew-day)** -- Everything from heating strike water to chilling your wort. This is the big one, usually 4-5 hours.
2. **[Fermenting](/process/fermenting)** -- Pitching yeast, monitoring fermentation, dry hopping, and knowing when it's done.
3. **[Bottling](/process/bottling)** -- Priming, filling, capping, and conditioning your beer.

# Websites

Useful sites to bookmark for recipes, calculators, forums, and general brewing knowledge.

---

### Brew Dudes
Recipe database, brewing guides, and equipment reviews. Straightforward and practical.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://brew-dudes.com)

---

### Brulosophy
Home of the exBEERiments -- controlled experiments testing common brewing assumptions. Also has great recipes and quick methods. Their "short and shoddy" brew day approach is worth reading.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://brulosophy.com)

---

### Brewer's Friend
One of the best all-around brewing tools. Has a recipe builder, water chemistry calculator, mash calculator, priming sugar calculator, and more. Free tier works fine for most things.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.brewersfriend.com)

---

### Ontario Beer Kegs (OBK)
Where to buy your ingredients and equipment in Canada. Good selection of 5 gallon recipe kits for beginners. Their recipe kit instructions are decent starting points.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.ontariobeerkegs.com)

---

### Brew In A Bag
Even though we're using a Brewzilla now, their site still has useful general all-grain brewing info and tips.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.brewinabag.com)

---

### BYO (Brew Your Own) Magazine
Long-running homebrew magazine with a huge archive of articles, recipes, and reference material. Their glossary is one of the most complete out there.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://byo.com)

---

### Homebrewtalk Forums
The biggest homebrewing forum. If you have a weird question or run into a problem, someone on here has dealt with it before. Great for recipe feedback and troubleshooting.

[!button text="Visit" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.homebrewtalk.com)

---

### Hop Reference Guides

- **YCH Hops** -- Detailed hop variety profiles and pairing suggestions
- **Yakima Valley Hops** -- Good variety descriptions and brewing use info

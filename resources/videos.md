# Videos

These YouTube channels are worth subscribing to. They range from beginner-friendly walkthroughs to deep-dive experiments.

---

### The Apartment Brewer

Great channel for small-space electric brewing. If you're brewing indoors or in a small space with your Brewzilla, this guy gets it. Lots of practical tips and recipe walkthroughs.

[!button text="YouTube Channel" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.youtube.com/@TheApartmentBrewer)

---

### The Bru Show

Solid homebrew podcast/show format. They cover equipment reviews, recipe formulation, brewing techniques, and interviews with other brewers. Good mix of beginner and intermediate content.

[!button text="YouTube Channel" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.youtube.com/@TheBruShow)

---

### Brew Dudes

Fun and approachable channel that doesn't take itself too seriously. Good recipe videos and brew day walkthroughs. They also have a great website (see [Websites](/resources/websites)).

[!button text="YouTube Channel" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.youtube.com/@BrewDudes)

---

### David Heath (DonOsborn)

One of the OGs of homebrewing YouTube. Tons of content covering every aspect of brewing -- recipes, equipment, techniques, troubleshooting. He's been at it for years and has a massive back catalog. Particularly good Brewzilla/Robobrew content.

[!button text="YouTube Channel" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.youtube.com/@DavidHeathHomebrewChannel)

---

### Brulosophy

The mad scientists of homebrewing. They run controlled experiments (exBEERiments) to test whether common brewing advice actually makes a difference. Does mash temperature really matter? Does dry hop timing change the beer? They test it and report the results. Eye-opening stuff that will help you stop worrying about things that don't actually matter.

[!button text="YouTube Channel" target="blank" variant="primary"](https://www.youtube.com/@brulosophy)

---

### Honorable mentions

- **Clawhammer Supply** -- Great production quality, good beginner-friendly all-grain content
- **Homebrew Challenge** -- Brews a different style every episode following BJCP guidelines
- **Short Circuited Brewers** -- Good electric brewing content

# Home

Welcome to your training.

Let's begin.

This guide covers all-grain brewing using the **Brewzilla 35L** all-in-one electric brewing system. The Brewzilla handles heating, mashing, and boiling all in one unit -- no propane burner, no separate mash tun, no brew bag. Just plug it in and go.

The homebrewing process generally involves the following steps (in order):

1. **Sanitization:** It is crucial to sanitize all equipment before brewing to ensure that no unwanted bacteria or microorganisms contaminate the beer.

2. **Mashing:** Fill the Brewzilla with your strike water, set the temperature, drop in the malt pipe with your grains, and let the enzymes do their thing. The built-in pump recirculates the wort through the grain bed for better efficiency.

3. **Boiling:** Lift out the malt pipe, crank the Brewzilla to a boil, and add your hops on schedule. A typical boil is 60 minutes.

4. **Cooling:** Use a wort chiller (or the Brewzilla's optional counterflow chiller) to bring the wort down below 80°F as quickly as possible.

5. **Fermentation:** Transfer the cooled wort to a fermentation vessel, pitch your yeast, seal it up with an airlock, and let it ride for 1-2 weeks.

6. **Bottling or kegging:** After fermentation is complete, the beer is either bottled with a small amount of sugar to carbonate it or transferred to a keg for carbonation and serving.

7. **Conditioning:** The bottled or kegged beer is conditioned at room temperature for a few weeks to allow the flavors to develop and the carbonation to stabilize.
